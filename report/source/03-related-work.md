# Related Work

Prior work in this area can be broadly grouped into two categories: approaches that
address problem A [@smith2023], and those that focus on problem B [@jones2022].

## Category A

Smith et al. [@smith2023] proposed an early solution based on heuristic search.
While effective in constrained settings, the approach does not scale to the
problem sizes considered here.

## Category B

Jones and colleagues [@jones2022; @brown2021] demonstrated that learning-based
methods can outperform classical baselines on standard benchmarks. However, their
evaluation was limited to synthetic data. Our work extends this line of research
to real-world conditions.
